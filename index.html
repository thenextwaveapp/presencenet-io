FAQ

Common questions.

Answers to the questions most clients ask before booking a call.

How much does an engagement cost?
Pricing depends on the scope of the work, and you get the full price in writing before anything begins — no hourly billing and no surprise change orders.
How long does a typical project take?
Audits are delivered within 2 weeks, outbound systems within 30 days, and custom software builds within 6 to 12 weeks depending on scope. A specific timeline is included in every proposal.
Who does the actual work?
The founder. Every engagement is scoped, built, and delivered by the same person you speak with on the discovery call, with specialists brought in only when a project requires it.
What happens after the project is delivered?
Every build ends with a documented handoff your team can operate independently. Ongoing support is available as a separate arrangement if you want it.
Do you work with clients outside Canada?
Yes. PresenceNet works with clients remotely and has delivered projects for teams across Canada and the United States.
What do you need from me to get started?
A discovery call or a written message. From there you receive a proposal.
