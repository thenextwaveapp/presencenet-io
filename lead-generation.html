Volume is easy. Booked meetings are the point.

Most cold email fails for boring, technical reasons: burned domains, weak lists, and no suppression, so messages land in spam and never get read. The system is built to fix those first.

The infrastructure runs across multiple domains and inboxes, warmed up and rotated, with suppression lists and reconciliation so nobody is emailed twice and deliverability stays healthy.

Messaging is researched and personalized in batches rather than blasted, because the goal is a booked meeting with the right person, not the highest possible send count.

  • Warmed sending domains and inbox rotation

A real outbound program

Selected work, described without client names.

A B2B outbound program that sent 5,000+ emails and booked 120+ qualified meetings in about three months, built entirely in-house: list building, sequencing, deliverability, and tracking.

Supporting infrastructure included suppression of 1,378 domains and 1,783 addresses, per-contact history logged across 210+ relationships, and a reconciliation tracker to keep results accurate.

FAQ

Common questions

How many meetings can I expect?
It depends on your market, offer, and list size, and no honest provider can promise a fixed number. As a reference point, one program booked 120+ meetings from 5,000+ emails in about three months.
Will cold email hurt my domain reputation?
No, when it is set up correctly. Sending runs on separate warmed domains and inboxes, not your primary domain, with suppression and monitoring in place to protect deliverability.
Do you provide the contact data?
Yes. Targeted lists are built around your ideal customer profile, then verified and deduplicated before anything is sent.
How is this different from a marketing agency?
You get a working system built and run by the person you speak with, with full visibility into the numbers, not a retainer managed by an account team.
What do I need to provide?
A clear picture of your ideal customer and your offer, plus somewhere for meetings to land. The rest of the system is built for you.
