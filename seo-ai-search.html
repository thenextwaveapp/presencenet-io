Search is moving. Your site should move with it.

Search is no longer just ten blue links. People increasingly get answers from AI assistants and Google AI Overviews, and if your site is not structured to be read and cited, you are invisible in that new surface.

The work starts with the boring foundation, because a slow, poorly structured site cannot rank no matter how good the content is. Speed, crawlability, and structured data come first.

From there, content is built around genuine buyer intent and formatted so both traditional search and AI models can quote it, which is how you earn visibility that compounds instead of visibility you rent.

FAQ

Common questions

What is AI search optimization?
It is structuring and marking up your content so AI assistants like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews can quote it directly in their answers. As people shift from search results to AI answers, this is how you stay visible.
How long does SEO take to work?
Technical fixes can improve speed and indexing quickly, but ranking and content gains typically compound over months. The work is built to keep paying off rather than spike and fade.
Do you do the technical work and the content?
Yes. The foundation, including speed, crawlability, and structured data, is fixed first, then content is built on top of it. Both matter, and they work together.
Can you help with Google Ads too?
Yes. Paid search often overlaps with SEO, and fixing account targeting and landing pages is frequently where the fastest savings and quality gains come from.
How do you report results?
You get clear reporting on rankings, organic traffic, and acquisition cost, so the impact of the work is measurable rather than assumed.
