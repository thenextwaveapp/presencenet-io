FAQ

Common questions about our services

What digital transformation services do you offer?
PresenceNet offers custom software development, B2B lead generation, SEO and AI search services, business process automation, data analytics dashboards, and IT cost savings. Engagements can stand alone or combine into one operating system for growth.
How much do IT consulting services cost?
Pricing depends on scope and is confirmed in a written proposal before work begins. Smaller tools and audits are typically a few weeks; larger custom software and SEO programs are scoped as discrete projects or retainers so you know the number up front.
Can one firm handle software development and lead generation?
Yes. Most clients hire PresenceNet because software, outbound, and search usually live in separate vendors. One studio designs the product, the pipeline, and the search presence so they reinforce each other instead of competing.
How long before SEO services show results?
Technical fixes can surface in weeks. Ranking and AI-citation work is a compounding program: most businesses see meaningful movement in three to six months when site structure, content, and authority are addressed together.
Do you work with small businesses and mid-market companies?
Yes. Typical clients are owner-led and mid-market companies that have outgrown spreadsheets, agencies on autopilot, or off-the-shelf software that does not fit how they sell and operate.
