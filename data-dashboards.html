Reporting people actually trust

Most reporting problems are not really dashboard problems; they are data problems. If the numbers underneath are inconsistent, a prettier chart just hides the mess. The work starts with the data model.

Once there is one clean, agreed source of truth, dashboards and automated reports are built on top of it, so every team sees the same figures defined the same way.

The aim is reporting that runs itself and that people believe, replacing the weekly ritual of exporting, pasting, and reconciling spreadsheets by hand.

  • One clean, agreed data model
  • Live dashboards in Power BI, Tableau, or the web
  • Automated, scheduled reporting
  • Consistent definitions across teams
  • Alerts on the metrics that matter
  • Documentation and training for your team

FAQ

Common questions

What tools do you build dashboards in?
Power BI, Tableau, SQL, and modern web dashboards. The right tool depends on where your data lives and who needs to see it, and it is chosen to fit your team rather than to lock you in.
Can you connect data from different systems?
Yes. Data pipelines pull from your various tools, spreadsheets, and databases, then clean and combine them into one consistent feed behind the dashboards.
Our numbers never match. Can you fix that?
That is usually a data-model and definitions problem, not a chart problem. The work starts by agreeing one set of definitions and one source of truth, which is what makes the numbers finally match.
Will reporting update automatically?
Yes. Reports and dashboards are built to refresh on a schedule or in near real time, so no one has to rebuild them by hand each week.
Do you train our team?
Yes. Every engagement ends with a handoff, documentation, and training so your team can operate and trust the system independently.
